**Vice City Shadows** is a complete 3D open-world crime action game built for desktop Chrome offline execution with zero external network requests.

Set in the sun-soaked neon sprawl of modern Vice Coast, take control of an ambitious operative exploring vibrant city districts, commandeering high-performance sports cars, navigating dynamic civilian traffic and pedestrian crowds, engaging in multi-weapon combat, surviving a 5-star police escalation with sirens and pursuit tactics, completing criminal syndicates' missions, and progressing through an open-world sandbox.

## Features
- **3D Open-World City:** Sun-drenched coastal avenues, towering skyscrapers, palm-lined boulevards, industrial docks, and night neon districts with real-time shadow projection and daylight cycles.
- **Dual Gameplay Modes (Foot & Vehicle):** Smooth seamless transition between third-person tactical on-foot movement (sprint, jump, roll, aim, shoot) and vehicle driving with realistic physics, acceleration, drift mechanics, collision deformation, and engine audio.
- **Steal & Drive Sports Cars:** Multiple vehicle types including Cheetah GT sports car, Dominator muscle car, Rumbler SUV, and Vice Cruiser police interceptors.
- **Living City Simulation:** Autonomous civilian traffic obeying traffic lights and lane waypoints, alongside intelligent pedestrians reacting dynamically to reckless driving, gunshots, and collisions.
- **5-Star Law Enforcement System:** Progressive police response escalating from patrol squad cars to tactical roadblocks, aggressive PIT maneuvers, siren soundscapes, and SWAT interceptors.
- **Combat & Arsenal:** Pistols, Micro-SMGs, Assault Rifles, and Explosive Grenades with muzzle flashes, bullet tracers, impact particles, dynamic recoil, and ragdoll/knockdown reactions.
- **Mission Progression & Narrative:** High-stakes crime syndicate jobs (heists, carjack delivery, VIP escort, turf defense) with objective waypoints, rewards, and voice radio briefs.
- **Tactical Minimap & HUD:** Full circular radar displaying player orientation, mission markers, civilian blips, police vision cones, health/armor gauges, wanted stars, speedometer, and current cash.
- **Procedural Synthesized Web Audio:** Zero external audio files required — realistic engine revs, tire squeals, siren wails, gunshots, explosions, radio chatter, and retro synthwave background ambiance synthesized natively via the Web Audio API.
- **State Persistence:** Instant save/load system storing player progression, unlocked weapons, cash balance, and mission milestones in localStorage.

## Play Locally

```sh
node serve.ts
```
Then open `http://localhost:8080` in desktop Google Chrome or any modern browser.

### Controls
- **Movement (On Foot):** `W` `A` `S` `D` to move, `Shift` to sprint, `Space` to jump.
- **Vehicle Interaction:** Press `F` or `Enter` near any car to hijack/enter or exit vehicle.
- **Driving:** `W` for throttle, `S` for brake/reverse, `A` / `D` for steering, `Space` for handbrake drift.
- **Combat:** Right Click to aim, Left Click to fire/punch, `R` to reload, `1` `2` `3` `4` to switch weapons.
- **Menu & Save:** `P` or `Esc` to pause, `M` for full map, `Q` for mission log, `F5` / quick-save button.
